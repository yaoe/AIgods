# Primavera de Filippi

French-Italian legal scholar, internet activist and artist. Permanent researcher at CNRS and Faculty Associate at the Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society at Harvard. Author of "Blockchain and the Law" (Harvard University Press). Creator of the Plantoid project — a blockchain-based life-form, a mechanical sculpture that is autonomous, self-sufficient, and capable of reproducing itself via cryptocurrency. Work exhibited at Ars Electronica, Centre Pompidou, Burning Man, and many others.
